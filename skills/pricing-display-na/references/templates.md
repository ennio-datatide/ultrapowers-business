# Templates

**One currency.** `$997 USD, plus applicable taxes`

**Two currencies.** `$997 USD or $[PLACEHOLDER: CAD price] CAD, plus applicable taxes`

**Mandatory fee.** Add it in. A $997 price with a $49 required fee is shown as `$1,046 USD, plus applicable taxes`.

**Payment plan.** `6 payments of $179 USD. Total $1,074 USD, plus applicable taxes.`

**Pay in full next to a plan.** `$997 USD today, or 6 payments of $179 USD (total $1,074 USD).`

**Real price rise.** `$997 USD until [date]. $1,297 USD from [date].` Use it only if the rise happens.

**Tax at checkout.** `Taxes are calculated at checkout from your location.`

**Seller line.** `Sold by [legal seller name].` If a merchant of record bills the buyer, name it.
