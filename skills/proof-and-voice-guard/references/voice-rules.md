# Voice Rules

**V1. Dashes.** Replace em dashes and en dashes with a period, comma or colon.

**V2. Contrast.** "Not X but Y," "it's not X, it's Y," "more than just X," "less X, more Y," and "not because X, because Y." State the positive claim directly.

**V3. Sign-offs.** Remove "I hope this helps," "feel free to reach out," "let me know if," and "just."

**V4. Reading level.** Sales copy at grade 5 to 7. Unbounce reports that grade 5 to 7 copy converted 56% better than grade 8 to 9. Benchmark from a vendor. Shorten long sentences, swap long words, cut clauses.

**V5. AI tells.** Flag these:
- Openers such as "In today's fast-paced world," "Whether you're X or Y," "Here's the thing."
- Stock verbs: unlock, elevate, supercharge, seamless, game-changing, leverage, delve.
- Triplets used for rhythm with nothing specific in them.
- Self-answered questions: "The result? 3x faster."
- Colon reveals: "The best part: it learns."
- Lists of what something lacks: "No setup, no fuss, no waiting."
- Sentences that restate the one before.

Buyers distrust visible AI marketing: in a Klaviyo and Datalily survey reported by eMarketer in December 2025, 7% said it raises trust and 31% said it lowers it. These figures come from the brief and were not re-checked.
