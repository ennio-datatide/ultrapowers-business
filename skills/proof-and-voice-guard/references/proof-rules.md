# Proof Rules

**Source file.** Keep a proof ledger: claim, source, date, owner. A claim with no entry is UNPROVEN.

**Statistics.** Name the source, year and what was measured. Vendor benchmarks are labeled as vendor benchmarks.

**Testimonials.** Real name or real initials, permission on file, original words kept, any free or discounted access disclosed beside the quote.

**Capability claims.** "Build a booking system in an afternoon" needs a recording or repo showing that build, and the time it took. Save the artifact before publishing.

**Comparisons.** Date them and link the evidence. Do not name a competitor without one.

**Placeholders.** If proof is missing, leave `[PLACEHOLDER: proof needed]` and say what to collect. Never invent a name, a number or a quote.
