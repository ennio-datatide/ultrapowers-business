# Email Briefs

**Warm-up 1, the useful one.** One thing they can do today. No pitch. Ends with one question that invites a reply.

**Warm-up 2, the work.** A real recording or artifact from the method. Link it. Say what went wrong too.

**Warm-up 3, the student.** One real story, with source and permission on file, and any free access disclosed.

**Warm-up 4, the date.** Enrollment opens on [date]. Who it suits, who it does not suit.

**Open.** Subject states the fact. Body: it is open, what they will build, the schedule, the price with total, the close date, one link.

**Proof.** One story or one objection. The objection comes from waitlist replies, in their words.

**Pre-start, 3 days.** The date, seats left from the live count, what the first session covers.

**Last call, 1 day.** Two short paragraphs. The date and the link. Say this is the last email about this cohort, and keep that promise.
