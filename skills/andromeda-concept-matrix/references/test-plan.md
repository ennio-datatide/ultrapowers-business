# Test Plan

Starting defaults from the brief. Replace them with your own data.

- Structure: one campaign, one broad Advantage+ ad set, 6 to 10 concepts.
- Optimization event: workshop registration.
- Starting benchmark: about $20 to $30 per lead. A rough figure from research; your data replaces it.
- Exclude Quebec unless a French funnel exists.
- Read results per concept: cost per registration, hold rate at 3 seconds, attended rate from the CRM.
- Judge concepts on attended workshops and enrollments, since cheap leads can fail to show.
- Replace the bottom third with new concepts. Make variations of winners with `ad-creative`.
