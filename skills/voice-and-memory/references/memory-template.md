# Business memory template

Saved as `.agents/business-memory.md`. Facts only, each with the date it was true. When a fact
changes, keep the new one and note the old one under History.

```markdown
# Business memory: [name]

Last updated: [date].

## The business
- What they do, for whom, where. [date]
- How they work: one to one, groups, online, in person. [date]

## Offer and prices
| Offer | Price | Format | Since |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Clients
- Who buys, and who does not. [date]
- **How clients talk:** their own words for their problem and for the result they want, quoted.
- Questions and objections that come up again and again.

## Launches and campaigns
| When | What | Channels | Result | What they learned |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Tools they use
[Email, payments, booking, social, accounting. What they like and what bothers them.]

## Goals
[What they want this year, in their words.]

## History
- [date]: [what changed, and the old value]
```
