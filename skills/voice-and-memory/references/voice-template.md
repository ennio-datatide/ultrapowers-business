# Voice file template

Saved as `.agents/brand-voice.md`. Fill each section from evidence; leave out a section with none.
Keep a short comment after a rule saying where it came from.

```markdown
# Brand voice: [name of the business or person]

Last updated: [date]. Sources: [ChatGPT export to date, N published pieces, custom instructions].

## In one paragraph
[How they sound, as they would describe it to a new copywriter. Concrete: who they talk to, from where
(an equal, a coach, a friend who knows), how long their sentences run, how much they joke.]

## We are / We are not
| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| [close, like a voice note to a client] | [chummy, slangy] |
| [direct: the price in the first lines] | [pushy: countdowns, false scarcity] |

## Rules
- [Rule, in their words.] <!-- corrected 3 times: 2025-03-12, ... -->
- [Rule.] <!-- custom instructions -->

## Words
- **Ours:** [words and expressions they use, and how they name their clients and their offer]
- **Never:** [words and phrases they corrected, plus their language's AI tells]
- **Spelling and format:** [tú or usted, emojis or not, exclamation marks, how they write prices and dates]

## By channel
### Instagram
[Length, first line, hashtags, emojis, call to action.]
### Email
[Subject lines, greeting, sign-off, length, one idea per email or several.]
### Landing pages
[Headline style, how they show proof, how they ask for the action.]
### WhatsApp
[How they write to clients one to one and in groups.]

## Examples
Real pieces, exactly as published or approved. Theirs first.

### [Channel] · [date] · [published | approved draft]
[The text.]
```
