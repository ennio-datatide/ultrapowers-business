# AI tells

Phrases and habits that make a text read as written by an AI. Add the ones in the person's language to
the "Never" list of their voice file, unless their own published texts use them (then they are theirs).

## Spanish
- Openers: "¡Claro!", "¡Por supuesto!", "¡Hola! 👋", "En el mundo actual...", "En la era digital...", "Hoy en día...".
- Filler: "Es importante destacar que", "Cabe mencionar", "Sin lugar a dudas", "En definitiva", "En resumen".
- Big words: "sumérgete", "descubre el poder de", "lleva tu negocio al siguiente nivel", "potencia", "revoluciona", "transforma tu vida", "un viaje".
- Groups of three adjectives in a row ("fácil, rápido y eficaz") in every paragraph.
- A question as headline, answered in the next line.
- Lists of exactly three where one or two would do.
- The em dash used for asides. Spanish uses commas, parentheses or the raya only in dialogue.
- Closing with "¿Y tú, qué opinas?" or "¡No te lo pierdas!" every time.
- English calques: "aplicar" for "solicitar", "en orden de" for "para".

## English
- Openers: "Absolutely!", "Great question!", "In today's fast-paced world", "In the ever-evolving landscape of".
- "Delve", "unlock", "unleash", "elevate", "seamless", "game-changer", "supercharge", "harness", "navigate", "tapestry", "testament to".
- "It's not just X, it's Y." "Whether you're X or Y..."
- Groups of three, em dashes for every aside, a rhetorical question then its answer.
- Ending with a summary that repeats the text, or "Let's dive in!".

## Habits, in any language
- Every paragraph the same length.
- Emojis as bullet points.
- Bold on a phrase in every paragraph.
- Hedging every claim ("can help", "may improve") when the person speaks plainly.
- Praise of the reader's question or situation before answering.
