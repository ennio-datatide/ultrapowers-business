# Pricing and Urgency

Checked against sources on 2026-11-01 unless marked.

## Price

**P1. All-in price in Canada.** FAIL when the advertised price cannot be paid because a mandatory fee is added later: booking fees, processing fees, platform fees, service fees. Only charges imposed by law, such as GST/HST, may be added on top. Basis: Competition Act s. 74.01(1.1) civil and s. 52(1.3) criminal, in force 23 June 2022, amended by Bill C-59 in June 2024. Also a risk in the US under FTC Act Section 5.

**P2. No fake "was" prices.** FAIL on a "was" or "regular" price with no record of real sales or a genuine offer at that price. Canada's s. 74.01(2) and (3) use a volume test or a time test: a substantial volume sold at that price, or the price offered in good faith for a substantial period. A new product that has never sold at the higher price fails both. Unknown history: NEEDS EVIDENCE.

**P3. Total cost of payment plans.** FAIL on an installment price with no total shown beside it: "$99 a month" with no "6 payments, $594 total."

**P4. Currency.** FAIL when a page shown to both markets gives "$" with no USD or CAD label.

**P5. Value stacks.** Stated values for bonuses need a basis, such as a real price sold at. "Worth $2,000" with no basis: NEEDS EVIDENCE.

## Urgency

**U1. Only true urgency.** FAIL on a countdown that resets, runs per visitor, or ends with no real change. Evergreen timers count. Deadlines, seat caps and price rises must be true and honored. Unknown: NEEDS EVIDENCE. Basis: FTC Act Section 5, Competition Act s. 74.01(1)(a).

**U2. Honor the deadline.** A cart reopened after a "final" close, with no new real reason, makes the earlier claim false. Check the plan as well as the copy.

## Subscriptions and guarantees

**S1. Auto-renewal.** Before billing, show the terms clearly, get express consent, and give a simple way to cancel. Basis: ROSCA and state auto-renewal laws. The federal click-to-cancel rule was vacated in July 2025, so ROSCA and state law still control. Lawyer review advised on state rules.

**S2. Guarantee as written.** The guarantee shown must match the refund policy and be honored exactly. Mismatch: FAIL.
