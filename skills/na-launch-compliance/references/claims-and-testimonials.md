# Claims and Testimonials

Checked against sources on 2026-11-01 unless marked. Rules change. Confirm the current text before relying on a figure.

## Earnings and capability claims

**C1. No income claims.** FAIL on any statement or implication of what a buyer will earn, in dollars or otherwise: "make $10k a month," "replace your salary," "quit your job," "six-figure business," "income on autopilot." Includes screenshots of revenue, and stories of a past student's earnings. Basis: FTC Act Section 5 and the FTC's Operation AI Comply actions (2024).

**C2. No promised business outcome.** FAIL on a guarantee or strong implication that buying produces a business, customers or sales: "build your own automated business," "get clients while you sleep." The approved rewrite is in SKILL.md.

**C3. Capability claims need substantiation.** Any claim about what the method or tool does needs evidence held before publishing. "Build an app in an hour" with no demo on file is NEEDS EVIDENCE.

**C4. Numbers need a source.** Every statistic, student count, rating or "as seen in" needs a source on file. No source: NEEDS EVIDENCE.

## Testimonials and reviews

16 CFR Part 465, in force since 21 October 2024. Maximum civil penalty $53,088 per violation (2025 figure, kept for 2026 after OMB cancelled the 2026 inflation adjustment).

**T1. No fake testimonials (465.2).** FAIL on a review or testimonial from someone who does not exist, including AI-generated ones, or from someone with no real experience of the product, or one that misstates their experience. An invented name or composite customer is a FAIL.

**T2. Disclose free or discounted access.** FAIL when a testimonial comes from someone who received free or discounted access, a gift or payment, and no clear disclosure sits next to it. Example: "Maria T., received free beta access." Basis: FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255), and Canada's Ad Standards guidelines for material connections. Part 465 itself does not set this disclosure duty, so cite the Guides. If the asset does not say how the person got access, result is NEEDS EVIDENCE.

**T3. No incentive tied to sentiment (465.4).** FAIL on an offer of money, a discount, a bonus or access in exchange for a positive review.

**T4. Disclose insiders (465.5).** FAIL on a testimonial from an officer, manager, employee or their relative with no disclosure of the relationship.

**T5. Typical results.** A testimonial describing an unusual result needs a clear statement of what buyers generally get. If you do not know, remove the result. A "results not typical" line alone does not fix it. Lawyer review advised.

**T6. No hiding negative reviews (465.7).** FAIL on a stated practice of suppressing negative reviews, or of showing selected reviews as if they were all reviews.

**T7. No bought social proof (465.8).** FAIL on purchased followers, views or engagement used to look popular.

**T8. Written permission.** Every named testimonial needs permission on file. No file: NEEDS EVIDENCE.
