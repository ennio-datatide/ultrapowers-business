# Privacy, Accessibility, Tax and Quebec

From the integration brief. Not re-checked against primary sources in this pass. Each item needs a lawyer's or accountant's view.

**L1. Quebec language.** Bill 96 requires French parity for commerce in Quebec. Exclude Quebec from targeting, or ship a full French funnel. A Quebec-targeted English-only funnel is a FAIL.

**L2. Quebec cookies.** Law 25 calls for opt-in to tracking cookies. Applies if Quebec visitors are served.

**L3. Privacy notice.** A privacy policy names the operator and what is collected. CCPA applies if thresholds are met. PIPEDA applies to Canadian personal data.

**L4. Accessibility.** Sales page, checkout, captions and player meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Not testable from copy: NEEDS EVIDENCE.

**L5. Canadian tax.** Non-resident sellers register for simplified GST/HST once sales to Canadian consumers pass CAD 30,000 in 12 months. A merchant of record can carry this. Flag only, for the accountant.
