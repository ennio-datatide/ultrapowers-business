# Asset Briefs

**Post.** First line names a specific problem. Middle shows the result on screen. Last line: "Comment [KEYWORD] and I will send you the link."

**Keyword.** One plain word linked to the topic. Easy to type on a phone.

**DM.** "Here is the link: [URL]. It is live on [date] at [time, zone]. Reply if you have a question."

**Registration page.**
- Headline: what they will be able to do by the end.
- Date, time with zone, length.
- Three things they leave with.
- Who is teaching, with real proof.
- Form: first name, email, unchecked consent box naming the sender.

**Confirmation.** Calendar file, what to bring, one question: "What do you most want to get out of this?" Answers feed copy.

**Workshop outline.** Five minutes on the problem. Most of the time on a live build. Ten minutes on the offer, stated plainly. Questions at the end.

**Replay email.** What they missed, the link, the real end date.
