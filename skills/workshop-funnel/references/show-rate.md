# Show-Rate Tactics

These are practitioner habits, and untested for your audience. Track your own numbers per step.

- Ask for a calendar save on the confirmation screen.
- Send a text reminder only with separate consent for text messages.
- Give a small prep task so they have already started.
- Promise one thing that happens live only: a live build, live questions.
- Pick the time by the audience's time zones. Run two sessions if the audience spans the US and Canada widely.
- Send the 5 minute reminder with the direct join link and nothing else.
- Measure registered to attended, attended to stayed for the offer, and stayed to enrolled.
